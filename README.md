Bypassing VAC: 2024 Update

Warning: Over the past two years, VAC has significantly tightened its security measures. While outright bans are rare, your Trust Factor might be lowered, leading you to play primarily with other cheaters.

Here’s our updated guide for 2024:

Original Instructions (2020-2022)
Our instructions from two years ago remain mostly relevant, but we've made some updates:

Manually Map Your DLL: Avoid using public downloads and source codes. Write everything yourself and do not share your hack.
Avoid VMT Hooking: Instead, use a regular detour/trampoline hook or a mid-function hook (not at the first byte of the function).
Use Human-like Features: Avoid obvious cheating. Don't rage hack.
Following these steps will significantly reduce your chances of getting VAC banned. While you can never be 100% safe, adhering to these guidelines makes you 99% safe.

Updated Instructions for 2024
Given the increased aggressiveness of VAC, here are the additional steps you should take:

Understand VAC Thoroughly: Familiarize yourself with the source code of Valve Anti-Cheat. A good resource is GitHub - danielkrupinski/VAC.
Dump and Reverse VAC Modules: Learn how to dump VAC modules and reverse-engineer them yourself.
Complete VAC Bypass: Create a comprehensive bypass by hooking all of VAC's scans/modules.
Additional Tips
Stay Informed: Follow the latest developments in VAC and anti-cheat measures.
Avoid Public Cheats: Using public cheats increases your risk of detection.
Regular Updates: Regularly update your cheats to stay ahead of VAC updates.
By following these steps, you can enjoy hacking CSGO with minimal risk. Remember, if you do get banned, you can always create a new account since the game is free.
